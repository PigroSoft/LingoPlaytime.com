Privacy

We don't know your child's name. Only your device does.

  • StoredYour child's name, age group, home language and the languages they're learning.
  • WhereIn this browser on this device. It never reaches us.
  • Not storedEverything else. No accounts, no logins, no email, no photos.
  • NoAds, tracking cookies, or social buttons. So there's no cookie banner either.
  • SoundsPlayed from files on our own site. Your child's name is never sent to a voice service.
  • CountingAt most a cookieless visit counter, so we know roughly how many people come by.
  • PayingIf you buy the app later, the payment company sees your email. That is kept apart from anything about your child.
