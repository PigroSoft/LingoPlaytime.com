Every guide is built around the same words as our games, so what a child hears here is what they hear while playing. They are written for parents of toddlers, and they work whichever of the two is your home language.

Questions parents ask

Which of these guides should I read?

The one for your two languages. The order doesn't matter: each guide is built for either language being the home language.

Do the games teach the words in these guides?

Yes. The 16 words come from our matching game, which plays in all six languages, and the other games teach many more.
